*Business requirements:*

The main objective of this project is to design a simple and easy-to-understand management dashboard for Manoj to
submit to his manager, Ravi, who comes from a non-technical background.

The dashboard will provide a clear overview of Sales, Profit, Products, Orders, Customer Order Status, Payment,
Fulfilment, and Geographic Performance in one place. It will present important business information through simple charts,
graphs, and KPIs, enabling Ravi to quickly understand the overall business performance without requiring technical
knowledge.

The dashboard will help Ravi monitor sales trends, profit performance, product and order performance, customer order
status, payment methods, fulfilment status, and geographic performance, supporting better business understanding and
decision-making.


**1) Overall Sales & Profit Performance**

*Business Question:
How is the business performing overall?*

**Dashboard should show:*

-Total Sales

-Total Profit

-Total Orders

-Total Units Sold

-Average Order Value

-Profit Margin %

-Monthly Sales & Profit Trend

*Expected Insight:*

*Manoj can quickly understand whether sales and profitability are improving or declining over time.*


**2) Category & Product Performance**

*Business Question:
Which products and categories are driving the business?*

*Dashboard should show:*

-Category-wise Sales

-Category-wise Profit

-Category-wise Units Sold

-Top 10 Products by Sales

*Expected Insight:*

*Manoj can identify high-performing categories/products and products that may need attention.*

**3) Order Status & Revenue Loss**

*Business Question:
How many orders are successfully completed and how many are being lost?*

*Dashboard should show:*

-Delivered Orders

-Shipped Orders

-Returned Orders

-Cancelled Orders

-Return Rate %

-Cancellation Rate %

-Category-wise Returns & Cancellations

*Expected Insight:*

*Manoj can understand the impact of returns and cancellations on overall business performance.*

**4) Payment, Fulfillment & Geographic Performance**

*Business Question:
Which payment methods, fulfillment methods and states are generating business?*

*Dashboard should show:*

-Sales by Payment Method

-Profit by Payment Method

-Sales by Fulfillment Method

-Profit by Fulfillment Method

-State-wise Sales

-State-wise Profit

-State-wise Orders

*Expected Insight:*

*Manoj can identify important markets and understand how different payment and fulfillment channels contribute to business
performance.*
